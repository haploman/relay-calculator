# Design differentiation plan

## Product

Relay Route Profit Calculator is a focused pre-acceptance route economics tool for owner-operators and small carriers. The rebuilt site exposes every deduction, includes deadhead in total-mile economics, and makes the decision threshold explicit.

## Visual direction

- Dense night-dispatch console rather than a generic marketing landing page.
- Amber is reserved for editable values and route context; green, amber, and red communicate the calculated decision.
- The page uses a horizontal route summary, three numbered input panels, one outcome band, and two compact analysis modules.
- Self-hosted Manrope and DM Sans create a technical but readable hierarchy without depending on third-party font requests.
- Tabler outline icons are used only where they improve recognition.

## Avoided patterns

- Use original branding and artwork throughout.
- No oversized lifestyle hero, invented testimonials, fake live-rate feed, or unsupported revenue claims.
- No gradient decoration, glass cards, or repeated calls to action.
- No hidden deductions: the formula and every cost category stay visible.
